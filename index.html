About the Property

A Home Blessed
with Grace

Lakshmi Nilayam is a thoughtfully designed residential building offering well-planned 1BHK flats in the heart of Siddapura, Bangalore. Named after the goddess of prosperity, every flat is crafted with care — generous ventilation, quality fittings, and a warm community atmosphere.

Situated just 100 metres from DMart Siddapura, residents enjoy unmatched convenience while living in a calm, well-connected neighbourhood. Whether you're a young professional, a couple, or a small family — Lakshmi Nilayam is built for you.
